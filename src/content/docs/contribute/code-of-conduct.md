## Standards

Zacess welcomes readers, students, educators, assistive technology users and developers at every level. Anyone taking part in any Zacess repository, discussion or issue is expected to be respectful, give specific and constructive feedback and assume good intent.

Many people here use assistive technology or have access needs of their own. Describe a barrier plainly, take a report of one seriously and never ask anyone to justify or prove a disability.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, abuse or personal attacks of any kind, including about disability. Spam or off-topic promotion. Sharing anyone's private information without consent. Posting documents you do not have the right to share.

## Enforcement

> **Note**
>
> Report unacceptable behaviour to [contact@zacess.com](mailto:contact@zacess.com). Reports are reviewed and appropriate action is taken, including removing content or blocking users where necessary.
